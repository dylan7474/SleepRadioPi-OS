Tune a Sleep Radio's speakers

  1. Put the laptop (or phone) about 50 cm in front of the radio, at speaker height, and keep the room quiet. Set the radio to a comfortable, fairly loud volume.
  2. On the radio's own page, press ⚙ Settings → Test sound → Pink noise, then straight away:

The suggested settings appear here.

Suggested

    Then on the radio's page: ⚙ Settings → Backup → Load settings… and pick the file. It holds only the speaker EQ and low cut, and the radio uses them at once. Or set the sliders by hand.

    Snapshots

    Play the same song (or the radio's pink noise) for each EQ setting, let the average settle, then save it. Click a snapshot's colour to hide or show it.

    Good to know

    A laptop mic hears little below ~100–150 Hz, so the deep bass reads low for everything. Typical laptop mic adds back a rough guess at what it misses; a measurement mic's calibration file (frequency and dB per line, like a UMIK-1's) is exact. Either way, keep the mic in the same place and the volume the same when comparing. The radio's pink noise skips its EQ and low cut, so it measures the bare box. The browser's echo cancelling, noise suppression and auto gain are turned off so they don't bend the curve. Made for SleepRadioPi-OS.
