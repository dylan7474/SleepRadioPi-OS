            Noise

            Balance

            Programme Noise

            The noise plays on top of whatever's on: the show, a station, an album or a book. In the middle of the balance both are at full level; slide towards one to turn the other down. The sleep timer fades and pauses the programme, not the noise, and pausing with the knob pauses only the programme: the noise stays on until you switch it off (here, on the Radio tab, or a preset button set to Noise on/off). Only on the radio's own speakers.

            Listen in a browser

            With it on, the main page gets a Listen here button that plays the show on your phone or computer too. Off, the page is just the radio's remote control, and the radio saves the work of making the stream.

            Test sounds and the knob

            Test sound

            To compare back panels: plays on the radio's own speakers at the current volume, with the EQ and low cut off, instead of the show for a moment. Turn the volume down first.

            Phase check: in phase should sound fuller and centred. If out of phase sounds fuller, one speaker's wires are the wrong way round at the amp (swap its + and −).

            Knob switch

            The same as pressing the radio's knob. Holding it for 3 seconds makes the radio beep and then read out its network address, or say it isn't connected.

            The back button

            Service menu

            The same as holding buttons 1 and 4 together for 5 seconds (which works on the Radio tab's buttons too, with two fingers). The DJ reads out the choices; answer with the buttons on the Radio tab: 1 restarts the radio, 2 resets the Wi-Fi, 3 gives a status report (then 3 again goes back to the previous version of the software), 4 is a factory reset (confirmed with 2, then 3). Doing nothing closes it.

            The DJ

            1.00×
            1.00×

            Changes are heard from the next gap between songs. Speed: 1× is the voice's own pace; a line or two may already be made at the old speed. Jingles only play on the main mix (all artists). Changing the voice restarts the radio (about a minute): only one voice fits in its memory.

                Wi-Fi

                    The radio's own network (hotspot)

                    The radio joins any saved network it can see. If it loses it (a router restarting, a weak signal in the night) it keeps quietly trying to reconnect, for as long as it takes, and never switches to its own network by itself. Its own network (the hotspot, for adding a network somewhere new) comes on when you ask for it with Hotspot below, or on a radio with no network set up at all: join it on your phone and open http://192.168.4.1. Holding the knob for 3 s says the radio's address.

                    This radio

                    This radio

                    What the DJ calls the radio, on the page and in the set-up Wi-Fi's name. Left empty, it's the type's own: Sleep Radio, or Phonosphere. The radio restarts to take it up (about a minute). (Recorded jingles are just files: swap them for ones with the new name.)

                    The grille light and the meter

                    The light follows the day and night sets of presets, and fades out on pause and with the sleep timer. Sweep the needle takes it up to full scale and back: turn the meter's trimmer so it just reaches the end. Meter reads moves where normal music sits (about 0 VU).

                    Updates

                    This radio: …

                    Updates come from the radio's releases on GitHub. The music carries on while one downloads; then the radio restarts (about a minute) and says how it went. If a new version doesn't come on air, it goes back to the old one by itself.

                    Buttons

                    Like a car radio's: press a button to play it (press again to pause). Set to now keeps what's playing on a button, the same as holding it on the radio for 3 seconds; or choose from the list. For an album, play it first. A podcast episode on a button starts a run: the radio plays on through the newer episodes, in order, up to the latest.

                    Two sets of the four buttons, each with its own presets. Swap them here, with the switch under the buttons, or on the radio by holding buttons 2 and 3 together for a second. With the clock on, the radio swaps at those times (quietly); a swap by hand lasts until the next one.
