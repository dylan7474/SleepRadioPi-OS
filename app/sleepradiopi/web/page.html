    The radio's own speakers only; saved on the radio. A boost lowers the overall level a little, so it can't distort. With the bass port back panel, set the low cut to 140 Hz. Or press Tune speakers…: it opens the spectrum analyser and the radio plays bursts of hiss for it to measure. Hold the phone about 50 cm in front of the radio and tap Start; it brings the settings back here for you to Apply. With no internet (e.g. on the radio's own Wi-Fi) it uses the radio's own copy, which needs a one-off Chrome setting on the phone: the page shows how.

    This radio has no speaker of its own (it only plays in a browser), so there's nothing to set here.

    Listen in a browser

    With it on, the main page gets a Listen here button that plays the show on your phone or computer too. Off, the page is just the radio's remote control, and the radio saves the work of making the stream.

    Test sounds and the knob

    Test sound

    To compare back panels: plays on the radio's own speakers at the current volume, with the EQ and low cut off, instead of the show for a moment. Turn the volume down first.

    Phase check: in phase should sound fuller and centred. If out of phase sounds fuller, one speaker's wires are the wrong way round at the amp (swap its + and −).

    Knob switch

    The same as pressing the radio's knob. Holding it for 3 seconds makes the radio beep and then read out its network address, or say it isn't connected.

    The DJ

    1.00×
    1.00×

    Changes are heard from the next gap between songs. Speed: 1× is the voice's own pace; a line or two may already be made at the old speed. Jingles only play on the main mix (all artists). Changing the voice restarts the radio (about a minute): only one voice fits in its memory.

    Voices

      Add a voice: an archive (.zip, .tar.gz, .tar.bz2 or .tar.xz) of a Piper / sherpa-onnx voice folder (a .onnx model, tokens.txt and espeak-ng-data). Then pick it as the DJ's voice above.

        Wi-Fi

            The radio's own network (hotspot)

            The radio joins any saved network it can see. If none is in range when it starts, it makes its own network (the hotspot): join it on your phone and open http://192.168.4.1 to add yours. Holding the knob for 3 s says how.

            Updates

            This radio: …

            Updates come from the radio's releases on GitHub. The music carries on while one downloads; then the radio restarts (about a minute) and says how it went. If a new version doesn't come on air, it goes back to the old one by itself.

            Password

            Forgotten it? Over ssh: sleepradio-password clear. The radio itself never needs it.
